Why rethink the trial

Employers don't trust the trial before day one starts.

Every number below came from WorkIndia's own data — 12,541 sales calls, a cohort of 98,505 employers, the ANN activation study, ten-plus first-week employer interviews, and a live walkthrough of the production employer flow.

10.3× higher conversion for employers who visit AND unlock a candidate, vs. the largest passive cohort
67% of agent-posted employers never open the platform at all — their whole relationship is WhatsApp and a phone call
91% of ₹1-trial first-time payers cancel the autopay mandate — most within the first hour
3× the trial's own pricing copy tells the employer it's the inferior option, before they've used it once
Live bug found

A fresh employer who just paid the ₹1 trial hits "Your wallet balance is low — buy a plan" on their own job post, seconds after checkout. Wrong copy for a trial-account state, reproduced live on production.

Interview insight

6 of 8 trial non-buyers who gave a reason cited candidate quality or responsiveness — not price, not trial length. Employers buy a hire, not access to a database.

Interview insight

Multiple employers assumed WorkIndia charges a placement commission, like a consultancy. It's a flat one-time fee — and nothing in the flow says so until a sales call corrects it.

The rethink

One trial, built around a hire — not a clock.

Click through the six moments below. Marking candidates on screens 2–3 updates the trial progress and conversion screens live, the way a real trial would track toward an outcome instead of a countdown.

Today vs. reimagined

What specifically changes, and why

MomentTodayReimaginedEvidence
Trial length Fixed 3 days, 5 leads/day, regardless of outcome Ends at 3 shortlisted candidates, 30 profiles reviewed, or 7 days — whichever first Interviews: employers judge by hire quality, not days used
Candidate review One click unlocks contact, no relevance check first AI pre-screens by voice (availability, pay, location, language) before the employer ever sees the profile; employer marks Relevant before contact unlocks Prior WorkIndia UAT: employers couldn't judge relevance from static fields alone
Calling Number revealed, calls happen off-platform Platform-only, masked, call must connect to count — no external dialing Enables the same call-quality analysis already run on 12,541 sales calls, now on hiring calls
Autopay mandate Signed for full price on day zero, before any value delivered Mandate stays (by design, to protect revenue) — but renewal is shown with evidence of what the trial delivered, not silently 91% cancellation rate; mandate itself is a fixed business constraint
Primary surface Web dashboard, rarely opened WhatsApp-first for agent-sourced employers; dashboard stays as the secondary view 67% of agent-posted employers never open the platform; WhatsApp Business API already live
Pricing framing Trial labelled "Limited reach / less relevant calls" against paid plans' "High reach" Neutral framing; trial sells itself on outcome, not on being told it's worse Found on pricing page, checkout, and plan card — three separate times
Post-hire No feedback loop between a hire and candidate quality WhatsApp check-in asking the employer to rate the hire once confirmed Feeds future "track record" signal and match-quality data over time
Open parameters, not fixed rules:
  • Batch depth (10 profiles/batch, 30 total) is an illustrative default — likely needs tuning by candidate-pool size per role/city, not a single number for every job.
  • A Relevant mark with no connected call doesn't block progress — the profile stays unlocked and retryable, pending validation this is the right default.
  • Whether "relevant" self-reporting needs a light objective check (e.g. requiring a connected call >60s) to resist gaming in either direction.
