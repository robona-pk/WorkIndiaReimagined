Pricing & hiring assurance

No surprises on cost or refunds

What you pay for, what happens if it doesn't work out, and how to reach us if something's wrong.

💰 How pricing works

  • ✅ Price depends on the role, city and number of hires — shown after you post, before you pay.
  • ✅ Hiring 10 or more people? Bulk plans are available at special pricing — talk to sales.
  • ✅ Pay by card, net banking, UPI or wallet. A GST invoice is emailed automatically.
  • ✅ Add your GST number any time from your profile, or ask support to add it.
Questions before you pay? 08069824660

🛡️ If it doesn't work out

  • ✅ A refund is available if WorkIndia is unable to fulfil your hiring requirement, per the published refund policy.
  • ✅ You can request a callback from support at any point during or after your plan.
  • ✅ WorkIndia serves genuine hiring businesses directly — not placement agencies or staffing consultancies.
Placement-agency and consultancy postings are not accepted, and payments from them are non-refundable — this policy protects the candidate pool for direct employers.
