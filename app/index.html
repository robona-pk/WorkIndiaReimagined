How the trial works

  • We surface fresh candidate leads for your job in batches of 10 — up to 30 in total — so there's always someone new to review.
  • Open a profile, check the details (skills, pay expectation, distance, AI call recording) and judge whether they're a fit.
  • Relevant? Shortlist them to unlock their number and call directly.
  • Your free trial covers unlocking 3 relevant candidates. Try to unlock a 4th and you'll be asked to subscribe.
